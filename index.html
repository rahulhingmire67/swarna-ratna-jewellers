Frequently Asked Questions

Are all gold ornaments at Swarna Ratna Jewellers 100% BIS 916 hallmarked? +

Yes. Every single piece of gold jewellery at Swarna Ratna is stamped with the Government of India's unique 6-digit HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) and tested under Karatmeters to guarantee 22K (916) purity.

How is the daily gold rate calculated at Swarna Ratna? +

We update our morning bullion rates daily for 24K pure gold, 22K standard gold, 18K diamond jewellery gold, and 999 silver in accordance with the official Indian Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA) market rates.

Do you offer custom bridal jewellery orders? +

Yes. Our master artisans craft bespoke bridal chokers, temple harams, and kundan sets customized to match your wedding attire, budget, and personal design requirements.

What is your lifetime gold exchange and buyback policy? +

We provide a 100% lifetime exchange guarantee at prevailing market gold rates for all hallmarked jewellery purchased from Swarna Ratna Jewellers with complete invoice transparency.
